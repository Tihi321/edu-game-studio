# EGS-02: course contract, item kinds and learning logic (phase 1, part 1)

Derived from the hub plan HUB-19 phase 1 steps 1, 2 (logic part) and 5. Branch: `EGS-02_course-contract`, stacked on `EGS-01_skeleton`. No language model: code and hand-written fixtures only.

## Goal

The language-neutral course contract every shell reads, its validator, and every pure-TS piece of learning logic the player and the lint share: item-kind checkers, tolerant matching, spaced repetition, adaptive difficulty and the seeded crossword / word-search generators. Plus the golden fixtures.

## Steps

- [x] `src/shared/course.ts` (zod 4):
  - `course/pack.json` (`CoursePack`): `schema` version, `id`, `slug`, `sourceLang`, `languages[]`, `subject`, `audience`, optional `cefr`, `units[]` (id, title key, objectives, `requires[]` unit ids = prerequisite graph), `objectives[]` (id, unit, Bloom level `remember|understand|apply|analyze|evaluate|create`, text key, `facts[]`), `items[]`, `shells` (`enabled[]` + `config` keyed by shell id, e.g. `config['quiz-arena']`; unknown shell ids allowed as `record<string, unknown>` so later shells such as `encyclopedia`, `mindmaze`, `rpg`, `roleplay`, `detective` extend without breaking), `assets[]` (id, kind image|audio, file, alt key), `cues[]`, `encyclopedia.articles[]` (id, title key, body key, `citations[]` -> `factbase` ids), `factbase[]` (id, source, url?), and an optional `extensions` record for later features (speaking items, teacher mode, multiplayer).
  - All visible strings are **keys** into the text catalog; answer keys are language-neutral (option ids, indexes, numbers, group ids, hotspot ids). Typed/cloze/listening answers whose accepted text is language-specific live in the catalog per language (`accepted` lists).
  - `course/text/<lang>.json` (`TextCatalog`): `{ lang, strings: Record<key, string>, accepted?: Record<key, string[]> }` - the only translatable document (Book Translator translates it like the VN pack).
  - Item kinds: a discriminated union on `kind` plus a **registry** (`ITEM_KINDS`: schema, `check(item, answer, ctx)`, `keyOf`, `forms`) so later kinds (speaking) register without touching the union consumers.   - Every item: `id`, `objective`, `kind`, `prompt` key, `explanation` key, per-distractor `why` keys (misconception explanation), optional `didYouKnow` key, optional `asset`, `difficulty` 1-5, `tags`.

### Design choices

- **SM-2**, not FSRS: no trained parameters, and exact intervals can be pinned. Grades again/hard/good/easy = quality 2/3/4/5. A fresh card with good x4: 1, 6, 15, 38 days. Plus an in-session queue: a miss returns after 2, then 3, then 4 other items (`recordAnswer`, `nextItem`), and a `mastery` stage (new, learning, young, mature).
- **Item kinds** (13): `mcq`, `true-false`, `multi-select`, `cloze`, `typed`, `match-pairs`, `order` (`timeline: true` shows the `when` labels), `sort-groups`, `hotspot` (circle, rect or polygon regions in image pixels; the answer is a region id or a click point), `listening` (`mode: choose | type`), `flashcard` (self-graded with a grade), `crossword`, `word-search`.
- **Registry**: `ITEM_KINDS` is a `Map<kind, ItemKindDef>`. A def holds `schema`, `form` (human label), `recall`, `check(item, answer, ctx)`, `keyOf(item)` (language-neutral key), `solve(item, ctx)` (an answer that checks right: what the bot plays), `textRefs(item)` (every catalog key used, role `string` or `accepted`), optional `validate(item, vctx)` (kind-specific integrity) and `distractors(item)` (wrong choices with their `why` key). The pack schema parses items as a loose envelope (common fields + `kind`); `validateCourse` then parses each item with its kind's schema, so a pack with a registered later kind validates (a `speaking` kind is registered in a test) and an unregistered one is `UNKNOWN_KIND`. Room to grow: `shells.config[shellId]` (any id), pack and item `extensions`, `schema: 1`.
- **Language courses**: pack field `targetLang`. The target-language catalog (`text/hr.json`) holds the phrases that are taught and their accepted answers; every edition resolves a key in its own catalog first and then in the target catalog (`makeTextResolver`). `languages` lists the editions only.
- **Matching policy** (documented at the top of `match.ts`): case, whitespace and punctuation insensitive; accents folded (č/ć -> c, š -> s, ž -> z, đ -> d, and `dj` read as d); a one-letter typo is accepted only when the accepted answer has 4 or more letters (`typo: true`); a swap of two letters is distance 2 and rejected; the accent policy is `ignore` | `warn` | `strict`, from the item's `strictDiacritics` or the course's `matching.diacritics`; under `strict` a missing accent is a near-miss (`diacritics: true`, not correct) and typos are measured with accents.
- **Crossword / word search**: the pack stores words (catalog keys with accepted lists) and a seed; the grid is generated per language (`crosswordFor`, `wordSearchFor`) over folded upper-case letters; the validator checks every word is placed in every edition (`PUZZLE_UNPLACED`, `PUZZLE_INVALID`). Crossword answers are compared as folded upper-case letters.
- **Validator codes**: SCHEMA, UNKNOWN_KIND, DUPLICATE_ID, UNKNOWN_UNIT, UNKNOWN_OBJECTIVE, UNKNOWN_ASSET, UNKNOWN_FACT, UNKNOWN_ARTICLE, UNKNOWN_OPTION, UNKNOWN_GROUP, UNKNOWN_REGION, MISSING_TEXT, MISSING_WHY (mcq, multi-select and listening-choose distractors), MISSING_GAP_MARKER, ASSET_KIND, PUZZLE_UNPLACED, PUZZLE_INVALID, MISSING_CATALOG, CATALOG_LANG, UNKNOWN_LANGUAGE, SOURCE_LANG_NOT_LISTED, plus PARSE and MISSING_ASSET_FILE from the loaders. Reusable by `edu_lint` (EGS-03): `findUnitCycle`, `itemsOf`, `formOf`, `ITEM_KINDS[kind].distractors` and `.textRefs`.

### Deviations from the plan text

- Units do not carry an `objectives` list: the link is `objective.unit` (one source of truth); `objectivesOf(pack, unitId)` derives it.
- The item schemas and the pack schema are strict (an unknown key is a SCHEMA problem); growth goes through `extensions` or a new `schema` number.
- The pack-level parse stops at its first problem (for example a bad `difficulty` on any item reports only that); kind-level, reference, text and puzzle problems are reported together once the pack-level parse passes.
- Added optional pack fields beyond the plan list: `targetLang`, `matching` (course accent and typo policy), `units[].summary`, `factbase[].claim`, `cues[].on/loop/volume`.
- `src/shared/edu/prng.ts` re-exports the existing Mulberry32 `SeededRandom` instead of adding a second generator.
- The fixture generator scripts (the bilingual source that wrote the JSON) were kept in the git-ignored `.claude/temp/`; the committed JSON is the source of truth from now on. Only the WAV script is committed (`scripts/make-fixture-audio.mjs`).

### Not done / for later

- Hotspot wrong regions and `sort-groups` items have an optional `why`, but it is not required (an `explanation` is).
